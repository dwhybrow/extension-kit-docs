This example shows how to move a published app from the **Start pages** section in the **Your employment** module, where is published by default, to another chosen location.

To move the app, follow these steps:

1.  Create an app.
2.  Publish the app. See [publishing and versioning](https://docs-external.u4pp.com/extensions-kit/app-studio/get-started/how-to-publish-app/) for more details. Your app appears in the **Your employment** module, inside the **Start pages** section. ![App in folder](https://docs-external.u4pp.com/extensions-kit/app-studio/images/tutorialsimg/tut5-step3.png)
    
    > Note: If your app does not appear, check that you have granted the necessary access rights in the **Menu access** window.
    
3.  Go to the **Custom menu items** window, located in the **Common** module inside the **System setup** section. You can also use the top-right search bar. ![Navigation path](https://docs-external.u4pp.com/extensions-kit/app-studio/images/tutorialsimg/tut5-step4_1.png)
    
4.  Expand the **Home** menu.
5.  Expand the **Your employment** menu to find your app. ![App in tree](https://docs-external.u4pp.com/extensions-kit/app-studio/images/tutorialsimg/tut5-step6.png)
6.  Select your app by clicking or tapping on it. ![Selected app](https://docs-external.u4pp.com/extensions-kit/app-studio/images/tutorialsimg/tut5-step7.png)
7.  Find in the tree menu where you want to place your app.
8.  Select an item inside the chosen folder. For this example: folder **Your development**, item **Course catalogue**.
9.  Right-click and choose the preferred option. For this example, _Place below_. ![Right-click menu](https://docs-external.u4pp.com/extensions-kit/app-studio/images/tutorialsimg/tut5-step10.png)
10.  Select the **Refresh** button in the top-right corner to see the changes. ![App in new folder](https://docs-external.u4pp.com/extensions-kit/app-studio/images/tutorialsimg/tut5-step11.png)
